# Clipfy 🎬

Clipfy is a modern, ultra-lightweight background clipping tool for Windows, built with Tauri (Rust) and React. It is designed to do exactly one thing perfectly: capture your best gaming moments without getting in your way. 

## Why Clipfy instead of Medal? 

If you play competitive shooters, you know that every frame matters. Mainstream clipping software like Medal has become bloated over time—packed with social feeds, heavy UIs, and intrusive advertisements that eat up your CPU and RAM. 

Clipfy is built differently. It strips away everything you don't need to give you pure performance. 

* **Zero Ads & No Bloat:** No social feeds, no premium subscriptions, and absolutely no ads. 
* **Minimal Resource Impact:** Powered by a highly optimized Rust backend, Clipfy runs silently in your system tray. It won't tank your framerate, keeping your high-refresh-rate gaming experience exactly as it should be.
* **Clean, Aesthetic UI:** A minimalist settings dashboard built with React and Tailwind CSS. You only see it when you need to change a setting.

## Features
* 🚀 **High Performance:** Adjustable video quality with support for up to 120 FPS recording.
* 🎧 **Audio Control:** Easily toggle your microphone (mute/unmute) for recordings.
* 🎵 **Customizable Notifications:** Choose from aesthetic built-in clip sounds or upload your own audio file so you know exactly when a clip is saved.
* ⌨️ **Global Hotkeys:** Bind your preferred keys to clip the last X seconds instantly.
* 🌍 **Multi-Language Support:** Available in English, German, Spanish, French, Italian, and Russian.
* 🛡️ **Failsafe Checker:** A built-in watchdog ensures your recording loop is always active in the background, so you never miss a clip.
